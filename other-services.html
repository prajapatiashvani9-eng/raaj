Comprehensive Specialized Services for Housing Societies

Raaj Society Solutions offers a wide range of specialized, legally compliant, and documentation-based services designed to strengthen the governance, financial transparency, and statutory compliance of co-operative housing societies.

Legal Compliance Framework

Our services are guided by the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960, Model Bye-Laws, RERA, MOFA, and relevant administrative circulars, ensuring accuracy, legality, and procedural integrity.
