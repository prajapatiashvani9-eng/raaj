Professional Statutory Management

We maintain, update, and verify all statutory registers and records as required under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960, the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961, and the Model Bye-laws of Co-operative Housing Societies.

Legal Requirements

Proper maintenance of statutory books is a legal obligation for every registered Co-operative Housing Society under Section 73, 79, and 165 of the MCS Act, and improper maintenance may lead to penalties, inquiries, and administrative actions from the Registrar.

We ensure that all statutory records are accurate, updated, and maintained in compliance with Government norms.

Statutory Compliance Services

Importance of Compliance

Regular statutory compliance helps societies avoid penalties, inquiries, disputes, and administrative actions under Sections 75, 79, 81, 146, and 147 of the MCS Act.

We ensure compliance with Registrar Circulars, Government Notifications, and all relevant statutory requirements.

Drafting of AGM / SGM / MCM Notices
