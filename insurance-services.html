Comprehensive Society Insurance Services

Insurance coverage is a statutory and essential requirement for the protection of Co-operative Housing Societies, their members, and assets.

Legal Requirement

Under the Model Bye-Laws (Bye-law 159), it is mandatory for every society to obtain and maintain suitable insurance policies for buildings, property, and liabilities.

Raaj Society Solutions assists societies and members in selecting, evaluating, and obtaining legally compliant insurance policies that safeguard financial, structural, and legal interests.

Society Building Insurance

As per Bye-Law 159, every Housing Society is legally required to insure its building against various risks and damages.

Coverage Required

Fire, Earthquake, Flood, Lightning, Structural damages, Natural calamities

We Assist In

  • Assessing building valuation
  • Selecting appropriate coverage
  • Ensuring inclusion of lift, water pumps, electrical systems, compound walls, and common areas
  • Claim processing assistance

This protects the society from major financial liabilities and rebuilding expenses.
