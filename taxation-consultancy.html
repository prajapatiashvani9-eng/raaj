Complete Tax Compliance for Housing Societies

Raaj Society Solutions provides comprehensive taxation advisory and compliance services exclusively designed for Co-operative Housing Societies, ensuring full adherence to the Income Tax Act, 1961, the Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, TDS provisions, and all other statutory requirements applicable to societies.

Our services help societies avoid penalties, comply with audit and filing rules, and maintain transparent financial governance.

Our Taxation Services

End-to-end tax compliance and advisory solutions for housing societies

Income Tax Registration & Filing

Complete ITR compliance for housing societies

We assist housing societies in all Income Tax–related compliance as per the Income Tax Act, 1961, including:

  • PAN Application / Corrections
  • Obtaining & Updating 12A / 80P Exemptions
  • Income Tax Return (ITR) Preparation & Filing
  • Computation of Taxable Income
  • Application of Section 80P Deductions for CHS
  • Responding to IT Notices & Scrutiny Assessments
  • Tallying records for audit compliance
Accurate declarations & maximized exemptions

TDS Registration & Filing

Complete TDS compliance and management

Housing societies are required to deduct and deposit TDS on various payments. We provide complete services including:

  • TAN Registration / Corrections
  • Monthly TDS Deduction Calculations
  • Preparation & Filing of Quarterly TDS Returns
  • Generation of TDS Certificates (Form 16A)
  • Correction Statements & Default Rectification
  • Ensuring compliance to avoid penalties
Timely compliance for all TDS obligations

GST Registration & Filing

Complete GST compliance for societies

Where applicable, societies must comply with GST norms. We offer comprehensive GST services:

  • GST Registration (if threshold exceeded)
  • Monthly / Quarterly GST Return Filing
  • Input Tax Credit (ITC) verification
  • Reconciliation of books with GST portal
  • Responding to GST notices and inquiries
  • Advisory on GST applicability
Full compliance with evolving GST rules
