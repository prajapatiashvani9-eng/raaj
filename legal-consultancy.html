Comprehensive Legal Solutions

Our legal consultancy services provide expert guidance and support for all housing society legal matters, ensuring compliance with relevant laws and regulations while protecting your society's interests.

We provide guidance and support as per:

  • Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960
  • MCS Rule 1961
  • Model Bye-laws 2014/2023
  • Society Disputes (Section 91, 101, etc.)

Services include:

Legal Documentation
  • Recovery notices (Section 101)
  • Drafting agreements and resolutions
  • Membership transfers & legal documentation
Redevelopment Advisory
  • Redevelopment advisory (as per GR dated 03.01.2009 & amendments)
Governance & Disputes
  • Guidance on elections, AGM, SGM
  • Handling disputes & compliance notices
  • Nominations & legal documentation

Our experienced legal team ensures your housing society operates within the legal framework, minimizing risks and resolving disputes effectively while maintaining compliance with all regulatory requirements.
