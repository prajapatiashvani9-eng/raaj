Complete Election Management for Housing Societies

Raaj Society Solutions provides end-to-end support for the Management Committee Election Process of Co-operative Housing Societies in accordance with the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960, the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961, and the Election to Committee Rules, 2014.

Legal Compliance Assurance

Our services ensure that every election is conducted lawfully, transparently, and efficiently, maintaining full compliance with statutory election procedures.

Management Committee Election Process

Fair and legally compliant elections

We assist societies in conducting fair and legally compliant elections, including:

  • Guidance on Type of Election (Contested/Uncontested)
  • Preparation and maintenance of Voters List
  • Coordination with Authorized Election Officer
  • Drafting of Election Notices, Agenda & Schedules
  • Assistance in Nomination, Scrutiny & Withdrawal
  • Support for Ballot Process / Polling / Counting
Strict adherence to election rules

Election of Office Bearers

Smooth transition of powers

After the formation of the new Managing Committee, we assist in:

  • Conducting the First Committee Meeting
  • Election of Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer
  • Drafting of Minutes and Resolutions
  • Submission of statutory forms to Registrar
  • Compliance with Bye-law No. 125 to 139
  • Smooth transitioning of powers
Complete legal documentation

Co-option of Committee Members

Legal co-option process

As per amended bye-laws and MCS Act provisions, our services include:

  • Identifying eligible members for co-option
  • Drafting resolutions for co-option
  • Updating statutory registers

  • Ensuring mandatory legal requirements
  • Compliance with MCS Act provisions
Follows legal requirements
