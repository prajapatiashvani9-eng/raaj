ABOUT ME

N. Gangadhar (Raaj Sir)

I am N. Gangadhar (Raaj Sir), a legal, accounting, and statutory compliance professional with more than 17 years of experience in serving Co-operative Housing Societies across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Panvel & Thane.

My expertise lies in providing end-to-end solutions in society administration, accounts, audit, taxation, legal advisory, documentation, and CIDCO-related matters.

Education & Credentials

I hold a B.COM, LL.B, LL.M, G.D.C&A & CA (Inter) and have in-depth practical knowledge of:

  • Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960
  • Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961
  • Model Bye-laws of Co-operative Housing Societies
  • MOFA, RERA & CIDCO Regulations
  • Municipal and Statutory Compliance Framework

Professional Background

With a strong foundation built through co-operative & district court practice and with senior legal experts, I bring a unique combination of:

  • Legal Expertise
  • Accounting Knowledge
  • Statutory Compliance Skills

This enables me to deliver professional, transparent, and fully compliance services to all types of housing societies.

Areas of Expertise

Legal Expertise

Deep understanding of Co-operative Society laws, regulations, and compliance requirements with practical court experience.

Accounting & Finance

Comprehensive financial management including billing, accounts, audits, and taxation for housing societies.

Statutory Compliance

Ensuring full compliance with all statutory requirements under MCS Act, GST, TDS, and other regulations.

Services to Housing Societies

Over the years, I have assisted numerous co-operative housing societies in:

Accounting & Billing

Complete financial management including monthly maintenance billing, ledger maintenance, and financial reporting.

Statutory Audits & Rectification

Comprehensive audit support and rectification of audit observations as per legal requirements.

Elections & Office Bearer Appointments

Guidance and support for society elections and proper appointment of office bearers.

Society Registration & Compliance

Assistance with new society registration and ongoing compliance with statutory requirements.

Builder-to-Society Handover

Smooth transition from builder to society management with proper documentation and legal formalities.

Conveyance & Redevelopment

Expert guidance on conveyance deeds and complete redevelopment process management.

Membership & Transfer Matters

Proper handling of membership applications, transfers, nominations, and related documentation.

Dispute Resolution

Effective resolution of society disputes through legal channels and amicable settlements.

CIDCO Permission, Transfer & NOCs

Assistance with CIDCO-related permissions, transfers, and No Objection Certificates.

Legal Drafting & Documentation

Professional drafting of agreements, resolutions, notices, and other legal documents.

AGM & SGM Meetings

Proper conduct of Annual General Meetings and Special General Meetings as per legal requirements.

Parking Issues

Proper guidance and solution of parking as per legal requirements & society bye-laws.

My Professional Philosophy

"Accuracy, Transparency & Legally Compliant Service Delivery."

- N. Gangadhar (Raaj Sir)

I believe housing societies need professional support that combines legal understanding with financial discipline, and my work focuses on ensuring societies operate smoothly, transparently, and within the legal framework.

With the establishment of Raaj Society Solutions, my aim is to bring awareness, professionalism, and accountability to housing society management and to help societies function efficiently with complete peace of mind.
