A

Annual Statutory Audit

The Annual Statutory Audit is compulsory for all co-operative societies to ensure compliance and financial transparency.

Legal Basis

Section 81 of the MCS Act, 1960 & Rule 69 of the MCS Rules, 1961

Our Annual Audit Includes

  • Verification of day-to-day accounts, cash books, ledgers, and vouchers
  • Examination of Income & Expenditure and Balance Sheet
  • Checking compliance with Bye-laws, Government Circulars, GRs, and statutory audit classifications
  • Authentication of investment records, bank reconciliations, and statutory registers

The final Audit Report is prepared in the prescribed statutory format and provided for filing with the Registrar.

Our Internal Audit Services

C

Financial Audit

The Financial Audit assesses whether the society's financial statements reflect a true and fair view as per accounting standards and co-operative regulatory norms.

Our Financial Audit Includes

  • Verification of assets, liabilities, loans, and advances
  • Review of capital expenditures, repairs, and maintenance transactions
  • Examination of statutory tax compliances such as TDS, GST, PF/ESI (if applicable)
  • Tracking deviations from accounting norms

This ensures accuracy, reliability, and legal compliance in society accounts.

D

Special Audit

A Special Audit is conducted when directed by Registrar of Co-operative Societies, Society Managing Committee, Investigating Authorities, or through member resolutions.

When Special Audit is Required

  • Suspicion of misappropriation
  • Financial disputes among members
  • Fraud, negligence, or unauthorized expenditures
  • Complex financial issues requiring expert legal auditing
  • Legal disputes requiring in-depth financial scrutiny

Special Audit Reports are drafted with detailed observations and evidence-backed findings in a legally presentable format.
